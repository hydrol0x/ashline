Your insurer wants a list of everything you owned. Your family’s photos already have it.

After a fire, a flood or a hurricane, text Ashline the pictures you already have: a birthday in the living room, last year’s holiday table, a listing photo from when you bought the house. It finds each thing, gives it an honest price range, and keeps one list the whole family adds to.

How it works

No app and no forms. Everything happens in the messages you already use.

  1. 01

    Send any photo from inside the home

    Old or new, blurry or not. Relatives join with a short code, and their photos land in the same list.

  2. 02

    Ashline circles what it finds

    Each item is numbered on your own photo with a price range. A couch seen in five pictures is counted once.

  3. 03

    You say yes or no, then send it

    Nothing goes on the list until you confirm it. Download the spreadsheet and the PDF your adjuster asks for, split by claim when a storm means more than one.

Built for the week after a disaster

Nothing is added without you
Ashline suggests. You confirm, fix or remove every item, and you can pause any time.
Your real photos, never edited
Items are marked on the photos you sent. Nothing is generated or invented.
A range, not a made-up number
Every price is a low-to-high range you can replace with your own figure.
One storm, two claims, sorted
After a hurricane, flooded rooms go to flood insurance at depreciated value and wind damage to your homeowners policy.
Questions for what no photo shows
For the garage or the hall closet, it asks a few short questions, one room at a time.
No insurance? Essentials first
It marks the appliances, furniture and computer FEMA can help replace, so they are easy to show.
