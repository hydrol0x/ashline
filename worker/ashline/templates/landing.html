How it works

Your family already has the evidence: holiday photos, birthday videos, old listing pictures. Ashline does the bookkeeping.

1

Text your photos

Send any photos from inside the home, from any year. Relatives can join with a code and add theirs to the same list.

2

Ashline finds and prices each item

Every item is circled on your original photo with a price range. Things seen in several photos are counted once.

3

Get the list your insurer needs

Review and confirm, then download a spreadsheet and a PDF, grouped by category, with the photo behind every line.

Made for a hard moment

Nothing is added without you

Ashline only suggests. You confirm, fix or remove every item, and you can pause at any time.

Your real photos, never edited

Items are marked on the photos you sent. Nothing is generated or invented.

Honest prices

Each item gets a range rather than a made-up exact number, and you can set your own.

Help remembering

For things no photo shows, Ashline asks a few gentle questions, room by room, at your pace.

Already started? Your private list has its own link. Text list to Ashline and it will send it again.
